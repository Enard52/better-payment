Add e-Fatura / e-Arşiv invoicing plugin (`invoicing`) with an official Paraşüt adapter: sales-invoice creation plus a separate, provider-confirmed issuance step (e-Fatura / e-Arşiv chosen by the buyer's registered inbox), required OAuth password-grant credentials, a pluggable `InvoicingStore` for legally-relevant records, automatic retries with failure hooks, and refund automation.
